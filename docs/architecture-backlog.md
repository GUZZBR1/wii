## Verified items that do not need new infrastructure

- The product has no server API, authentication or remote storage. Do not create API/repository layers or custom permissions without a product need.
- The `@nevergear/spatial-core` workspace, its test build to `dist` and the Vite source alias in the app serve different purposes; the current audit found no duplication that justifies removing them.
- `cameraPip.test.ts` and `cameraPip.test.tsx` verify different contracts (markup and DOM state/interaction); they are not proven duplicates.
- Worktrees with local changes were inspected read-only. No incomplete implementation was copied to this branch.
