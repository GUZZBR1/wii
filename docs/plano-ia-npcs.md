# PRD and implementation plan: opponents in the three sports

Status: **ready for review before implementation; local prototype rejected in review**. Research and source limitations: [pesquisa-ia-npcs.md](pesquisa-ia-npcs.md). Date: November 28, 2026. The observed remote `main` is `f001f6d`; boxing PR #52 was integrated into it. The `research/sports-npc-ai` worktree contains uncommitted local changes from an earlier attempt: preserve them for reference, but reassess every hunk against this document before reuse. A green test from that attempt does not prove the criteria below.

## Product and success criteria

Deliver **a single PR** against `santleme/wii:main` for tennis, boxing, and athletics. Opponents must make coherent, distinguishable choices; respond only to what they could perceive; obey the game’s physical and temporal rules; and remain accessible with a webcam. A player action must produce an outcome that can be understood from the displayed position, gesture, and cues. Do not promise perfectly human AI: claim an experience improvement only after comparing it with players.

The deliverable does not change the pose pipeline or add a backend, uploads, habit persistence, external data, an inference dependency, or a trained model. Change current rules and assistance only when preserved by tests and calibrated against the baseline. The PR must be independent of open PRs; at the start, verify `origin/main`, status, and diff, and update the branch without discarding local work. Work in reviewable commits, but publish one PR. Do not remove prior documents or work without evaluating it and preserving useful content.

## Local prototype review: blockers before any delivery

| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 tennis | `TennisGame.ts` reconstructs the ball in `applyPlayerOutcome`, but not the NPC’s movement; later contact uses the current `opponentX` for the reconstructed instant. Normal update order also moves the avatar before the ball without interpolating to the contact instant. | The same swing with immediate and delayed confirmation, within the rollback window, produces the same trajectory, contact position, decision, and score; contact interpolated mid-tick does not use a future position. |
| P0 tennis | The local check uses only X; for example, a ball at `(0,10,-6.2)` with velocity `(0,-1,-1)` can cross the plane at ~4.1 m high and still pass the lateral check. A random failure on a reachable ball freezes the NPC without a visible error gesture. | No contact outside lateral, longitudinal, or vertical reach; a reachable ball that is missed has a visible cause and scores once. |
| P1 tennis | Prediction starts only after the first serve bounce; local latency begins there. Aiming reads current `playerX`, and integration uses `Math.random`. Removing the three guaranteed returns and point-by-point adjustment was done without calibration. | Serves observed in the air and after a bounce have coherent timing; a seed reproduces targets; assistance is explicit and the opening experience is compared with the baseline. |
| P0 boxing | In `opponentAi.ts`, punch type and target determine whether slip/duck works, but `opponentAnimationState`/`poseGloves` receive only state and hand. Players cannot read distinct defenses. The player’s guard in `applyOpponentPunch` does not distinguish the target; choosing a body shot against a guard therefore does not create an effective opening. | Every attack whose defense differs has a visually and temporally distinguishable cue before impact; the declared matrix produces outcomes consistent with guard and reach. |
| P0 boxing | `applyLatePunch` discards `kind` and `target` received by `landPunch`; a typed punch confirmed after the bell loses the handling applied during the round. | For the same pre-bell peak, typed punches confirmed before/after the bell produce equivalent damage, result, and adjudication, including at the end of a fight. |
| P1 boxing | The lateral dash moves the NPC, but player punch adjudication checks radial reach and guard/opening without checking angular/temporal evasion. The attempt’s delayed reaction covers punch selection, but `moveFighters` uses current position and punch. | The same punch hits without a dodge and misses when the dodge actually reaches; locomotion or tactics cannot react before the configured delay. |
| P1 boxing | The local `lets the player step out of an opponent punch` test was weakened in the attempt: HP alone does not prove an attack or evasion occurred. `RunGate` allows up to 1 s without pose before pausing; missing data is not a low guard. | Force the attack phase and confirm impact plus evasion feedback; loss/recovery within and outside the tolerance never feeds memory as a negative sample. |
| P1 athletics | `standings` displays projected times for rivals still running as if they were final results. The local formula extrapolates current speed, which changes throughout the race. | P2 tests | The local tennis reach test checks only a numeric comparison; the race timing test uses the exit speed itself, making it weakly independent. | Integration scenarios verify cause, contact, and ordering; race is also checked against an independent numeric fixture over multiple steps. |

Code-review findings are concrete risks, not proof of their frequency in real matches. There is no webcam recording or latency benchmark quantifying how often they occur.

## Architecture and interfaces

- **Sport-specific simulation, not a universal framework.** Each pure controller receives `previousState`, `observation`, `dt`/simulation time, and an injected RNG; it returns new state, intent, and events, without DOM, Three.js, global clock reads, or implicit randomness. The game owns the state; rendering and audio consume results and do not adjudicate hits. Use sport-specific functions and types until actual reuse exists.
- **Perception:** immutable snapshots with simulation-clock timestamps, per-field validity, and bounded history. The NPC sees only samples up to `now - reaction`; the same delayed data drives decisions and movement. `unknown`, lost tracking, and stale samples do not mean no action. Do not infer punch-type confidence from detection confidence: the classifier has confidence that the current interface does not propagate; initially learn only signals that are already reliable, without changing the worker protocol.
- **Policy:** filter illegal actions before scoring, use finite named scores per action, resolve ties with an injected RNG, and keep parameters fixed by profile. Commitment and recovery prevent frame-to-frame toggling. Profiles define preferences and limited capabilities; do not grant future vision, teleportation, or guaranteed hits. Difficulty stays fixed during a match. Session memory records only confirmed events, with a minimum count, smoothing, and capped influence; reset it for a new match, preserve it through its points/rounds, and freeze it during pauses. Do not interpret a missing event as a defense or error.
- **Time and replay:** document the owner, reads/writes, bounds, and invalidation of each history. A real pause freezes simulation; in boxing, tracking tolerance follows the contract below. For tennis reconstruction, store snapshots of the ball, phases, serve, score, faults, both players’ positions and velocities, observations, policy, serializable RNG state, pending events, and player inputs with timestamps. Restore the snapshot at the accepted event time and replay everything together to the present, with stable order: inputs, movement, contact, and scoring; rendering randomness uses a separate stream. Keep a confirmation watermark: final score and result audio remain pending during the already-accepted late-input window; when it closes, publish once and reject inputs that would change a confirmed result. Early visual effects are cosmetic only. Prune snapshots at the watermark and clear them when the rally changes. Equivalence is required for identical inputs, timestamps, and seed within the accepted window. Test ribbon boundaries, watermark, pause, and result delay; do not silently shorten the already-accepted window. Measure cost and budget before extracting a collaborator; current code that publishes the player’s points immediately must change to respect this boundary.
- **Initial values:** 200 ms initial reaction is a prototype parameter, not a human measurement; keep other existing limits and assistance as a starting point, then calibrate by scenario and simulation. Do not import elite-athlete numbers literally.

## Behavior by sport

### Tennis

When observing the ball, generate candidate contact windows from a trajectory predicted using available observations (deterministic physics prediction is allowed; knowledge of future player actions is not), respecting the mandatory first serve bounce, net, and scoring rules. Movement can anticipate before the bounce, but contact cannot be illegal. Integrate acceleration, speed, and recovery on court; check X/Z position, ball height, racket reach, and action state at the exact instant. Animation follows the same state. If the NPC cannot reach the ball, do not schedule a return; if it can reach it under pressure, choose a safe or risky shot with a visible, coherent execution and miss. Choose placement from observed space and risk, without reading the player’s future position. Do not freeze the NPC to represent an error without a visual explanation.

Keep the first three returns assisted in the opening behavior while measuring the baseline: assistance may reduce error risk **only when contact is physically possible**. The guarantee no longer covers physically unreachable balls: that is an explicit change requiring comparison. Without a human test, keep the guarantee for all reachable contacts and note that the accessibility of unreachable balls remains unvalidated. Keep the existing adaptation until an accessibility comparison exists; with that evidence, replace it with fixed capability chosen by the profile. If comparison is not possible, preserve the behavior and track the difficulty debt separately. The NPC must not simulate more than one outcome for the same contact.

### Boxing

A timestamped observation contains player and opponent positions, lane/validity state, guard, duck, and identified punch onset, each with independent validity. Change app→game wiring and internal types to preserve timestamps, source, and `undefined` for controls; do not change the worker or detector. Virtual geometry and keyboard input are valid according to simulated state; camera signals are valid only when their source provides them. The same delayed observation drives movement intent and policy, while collision, ropes, and animation use the NPC’s current physical position. During `RunGate`’s 1 s tracking tolerance, the fight and round clock continues: do not start a new windup when required signals are unknown, do not learn from them, and let an already prepared punch resolve under the existing rule; unknown guard and duck use the existing adjudication without being classified as habits. When actually paused, freeze both clocks and invalidate camera samples from before tracking loss; after return, require new valid samples. In keyboard-only mode, do not block AI for lack of a camera. Preserve peak compensation, the 450 ms window, and unique events.

Run guard → preparation → impact → recovery states; choose approach, distance control, defense, attack, and counterattack only when valid. An attack requires observed reach at startup and reach revalidation at impact. Do not change type/target after preparation; keep enough phase history to answer the full 450 ms window, even in short sequences. Use combos of at most two punches, with new cues and a reaction window for each punch; if this cannot be signaled unambiguously, keep isolated punches in the first PR and document the gap instead of simulating an invisible combo.

Before differentiating defenses, extend animation or add explicit cues during windup so both type and target are identifiable at normal speed; if that is not possible, unify defenses for visually indistinguishable attacks. Give guard the same effect against every target while the current rule is singular; do not sell body aiming as exploiting a high guard. The NPC’s evasion needs spatial state and a time window that affect received-punch adjudication, without general invulnerability. Fix typed punch loss when a pre-bell punch is confirmed after the bell as an independent regression in the PR. For an NPC attack whose impact precedes the bell but is still within the 150 ms grace period, freeze the round clock at the bell and defer its resolution and final result until the defense window completes in simulation time; order defenses and pre-bell punches by timestamp, with stable tie-breaking, and publish the result once. Attacks with impact after the bell are canceled. Test round and fight endings. Rookie, aggressive, and technical profiles keep separate pressure, risk, and defense parameters, not just colors and timing.

### Athletics

Each rival has a fixed per-race profile for start reaction, acceleration, target speed, and gradual slowdown at the end; small start variations derive from the seed without unfairly changing classification mid-race. Use distinct curves for 100/200/400 m, qualitatively inspired by reports and calibrated to current arcade times; no reaction to the player’s position and no mandatory final sprint. Keep speed finite and non-negative; record finish once, interpolated within the tick, and keep post-finish motion visually limited without changing the time.

Extend the internal standings type and HUD contract with a `confirmed`/`estimated` discriminant; the screen explicitly identifies estimated times while a rival is still running and does not present the ranking as final. If a rival finishes later, replace its estimate with a confirmed time; reorder by estimate while any remain pending and by actual time once all have finished; confirmed times are never recalculated. Keep `place` based on distance during the race and on finish times afterward. Preserve records, restart, and rounding.

## Execution and validation

1. **Before editing:** record the diff and baseline against `origin/main`; reassess/revert only prototype hunks that conflict with this PRD, preserving user work. Measure scenarios for each sport and record conditions, seeds, and baseline values. Add tests that fail before the fixes above.
2. **Tennis:** extract the controller and temporal history; implement coherent contact and replay before tuning tactics. Test bounce, out, net, serve/fault, height, plane, reach, rollback, detector delay, time-window boundary, pause, reset, seed, scoring, and one-time effects. Compare rally frequency and duration with the baseline.
3. **Boxing:** fix post-bell type regression and cues; integrate delayed perception, movement, and adjudication; then introduce profiles and memory. Test delay boundaries, unique punch events, pose loss/recovery within and beyond tolerance, the 450 ms window across phases, bell, actual evasion, block, reach, animation and visible cues, pauses, and three reproducible profiles.
4. **Athletics:** implement profiles and identified estimates; test all three distances, monotonicity, finiteness, start, interpolated finish against an independent fixture, multiple steps, seed, tie, rivals still running, and long waits after finish. Compare time and placement distributions with the baseline before choosing final limits.
5. **Integrate:** run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, and `npm run build` on Node 24; record skipped tests, warnings, and recording availability. Compare at least 100 paired seeds per scenario, with identical inputs and seeds in the baseline and change, for distribution inspection, not as a player study. Measure p50/p95/p99 update and frame time on identified hardware/browser with camera/MediaPipe active, plus pose latency using the existing bench; compare baseline and change on the same setup. If no camera, hardware, or participants are available, report validation as pending without claiming a proven improvement or performance gain.
6. **Human evaluation:** when possible, counterbalance old/new order with players of different skill levels; record perceived difficulty, fairness, readability, and comfort as well as outcomes. Report sample size and limitations. Do not claim statistical significance from a small pilot. The constraint that contact must be physically possible is an explicit assistance change even without a human test; keep the guarantee for reachable contacts and note the limitation. Any additional change to assistance or difficulty requires human comparison or preserves existing behavior.
7. Review the diff and risks, confirm the PR has only this purpose, describe before/after scenarios, tests, and limitations, and open one PR against `santleme/wii:main` when automated gates and P0 fixes pass. Camera or human validation that was unavailable must be explicitly marked in the PR; do not imply it was completed.

## Review result and decisions

**Result:** plan for implementation; current changes are a prototype, not an accepted implementation. **Evidence:** code inspection, independent tennis/boxing review, baseline documentation, and cited sources. **Gaps:** listed integration tests, webcam, calibration, performance measurements, and human evaluation. **Publication blocker:** fix and test all P0 items; lack of a webcam does not allow claiming that validation was completed. **Confidence:** high for inconsistencies directly traced in code, medium for the proposed architecture, and low for parameters and perceived effects until measured. **Next action:** start implementation from the baseline and regressions using this document, without automatically including the prototype in the PR.
